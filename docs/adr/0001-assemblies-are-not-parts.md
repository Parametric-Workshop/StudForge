# Assemblies are not parts

Rebrickable gives factory-combined assemblies, such as a wheel with its tire or a hinge with both halves, their own part numbers. Some set inventories list the assembly, while others list its individual parts. We treat an assembly as a group of parts, not as a part in its own right. Its usage counts toward each part it contains, and it counts as covered only when all of those parts are covered. That way the same physical piece is counted once, however a set happens to list it.

## Consequences

StudForge's part counts and usage figures will not match Rebrickable's raw numbers. Assembly part numbers are kept in the Rebrickable Census for reference, but they have no usage of their own.
