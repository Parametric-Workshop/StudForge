# Shapes are keyed by Rebrickable part number

Every StudForge shape and decorated shape is identified by its Rebrickable part number, such as `3069b` or `3069bpr0001`. BrickLink, LEGO and LDraw numbers are kept only as cross-references, so search can still find a shape by any of them. We chose Rebrickable because it is the most complete parts database that is free to download in bulk, for personal and commercial use with credit, with official set inventories and mappings to the other numbering systems. BrickLink is probably more complete, but its catalog needs an account to download and its terms limit redistribution. LEGO gives one shape several design IDs, one per mold revision, so its numbers can't identify a shape on their own.

## Consequences

Part numbers appear in file names, coverage records and, once hosted, page addresses, so changing the key later would touch all of them. A shape with no Rebrickable part number gets a StudForge-only ID, decided when the first such shape comes up.
